# My Pace Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 24, 2026**

This privacy policy applies to My Pace Fitness, My Pace Body, and My Pace Nutrition, including their widgets.

## Data Collection

The My Pace apps do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The apps do not include advertising or tracking technologies.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Workout information, body records, progress photos, nutrition information, goals, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Some information may be shared locally between My Pace apps and widgets installed on the same device using Apple's app-group storage. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

## My Pace Fitness

With your permission, My Pace Fitness can connect to compatible Bluetooth heart-rate devices. Heart-rate information is processed locally and may be stored on your device as part of your workout history. It is not transmitted to the developer.

The app may also use local notifications and alarms for workout timers and reminders. Notification information is processed on your device.

## My Pace Body

With your permission, My Pace Body can access the camera or photo library when you choose to create or select progress photos. Photos and body records remain on your device and are not transmitted to the developer.

## My Pace Nutrition

Meal entries, nutrition goals, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

## Children’s Privacy

The My Pace apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Data Control and Deletion

You can manage or delete information using controls provided within the apps. Deleting all My Pace apps removes their locally stored data, subject to Apple's device, backup, and storage behavior.

Because the developer does not collect or store user data on external servers, there is no server-side user data to request or delete.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the apps' features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
