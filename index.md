# My Pace Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 24, 2026**

This privacy policy applies to My Pace Fitness.

## Data Collection

My Pace Fitness does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The app does not include advertising or tracking technologies.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Workout plans, workout history, preferences, and related information are stored locally on your device. Some information may be shared locally with My Pace widgets or other My Pace apps installed on the same device using Apple's app-group storage.

This information is not transmitted to the developer.

## Bluetooth and Heart-Rate Information

With your permission, My Pace Fitness can connect to compatible Bluetooth heart-rate devices. Heart-rate information is used locally to provide workout features and may be stored locally as part of your workout history. It is not transmitted to the developer.

## Notifications

With your permission, the app uses local notifications and alarms for workout timers and reminders. Notification information is processed on your device.

## Children’s Privacy

My Pace Fitness does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Data Control

You can manage or delete information using the controls provided in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to Apple’s handling of data shared with other installed My Pace apps or widgets.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or privacy practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
